## Resource-specific interpretation

Unlike the preceding workbooks, this sheet uses only A:K. G1 is blank but G2:G227 contains the source ID, H=`best hit`, I=`%ID`, J=`CYP name`, and blank-headed K contains the literal protein sequence. Rows 2–221 have a CYP name and are accepted. Rows 222–227 have blank H/I/J classification cells and are excluded unnamed candidates. Source `P` suffixes, `X`, `O`, and terminal `-` characters are preserved and flagged. No sequence is repaired or deduplicated.
