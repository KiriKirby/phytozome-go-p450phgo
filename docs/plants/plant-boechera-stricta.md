## Resource-specific interpretation

This workbook has one sheet named `Sorted by CYP name`. For this file, H=`seq ID`, I=`best hit`, J=`%ID`, K=`CYP name`, and blank-headed L is the literal protein sequence. Rows 2-228 are named and accepted. Rows 229-234 have empty I/J/K classification cells and are excluded unnamed candidates even though H and L contain data. Source P suffixes and literal O, X, and gap characters are retained and flagged. Two groups of identical literal sequence remain separate because each row has its own ID. No sequence is repaired, translated, or deduplicated.
